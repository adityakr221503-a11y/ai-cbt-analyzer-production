Privacy

RankForge is designed with a student-first, local/offline-first approach.

Study data

Questions, attempts, history, mistakes and practice state may be stored locally in the app/browser so the student can continue using the app.

Control

The app includes local data-reset controls. Do not enter passwords, payment information or other sensitive information into ordinary study fields.

AI features

Some AI-powered features may communicate with a configured AI service. The exact data sent depends on the feature being used.
